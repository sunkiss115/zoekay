Lesson Format

I offer tutoring sessions via Google Meet, in person in Marin, or a flexible combination of both.
I provide practice materials tailored to each student’s learning style and goals, and each student has access to a review resource they can use after our sessions.
I always accommodate students’ learning profiles and needs in our tutoring sessions and in the resources that I provide.
I am committed to creating a supportive and effective learning experience for every student.

Individual Sessions

Standard sessions are one hour for $100.

Sessions can be lengthened and prorated accordingly.

Group Sessions

Students can choose to study with one or more classmates, which is a particularly helpful option prior to an assessment!

Each additional student is $50/hour.

Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

  • Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance. Cancellations and reschedulings made with less than 24 hours' notice will incur 100% of the session fee.
  • No-shows: If a student does not show up within 15 minutes of the start time without notice, the session will be billed as a full-fee cancellation.
  • Emergencies: In the event of an emergency or power failure (winter in California!), please try your best to communicate with me as soon as possible so we can reschedule.

Billing

Invoices will be sent at the end of each month and can be paid via Venmo or Zelle. Alternative payment methods can be used if online payment is not preferred.

Teacher & School Collaboration

If it is helpful during the school year, I am happy to collaborate with the student's teacher via email, Canvas, or a similar LMS.

Please reach out with any questions or for any details not mentioned above!
