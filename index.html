About

¡Bienvenidos!

I have been a language educator for over 20 years and I teach both Spanish and English to learners of all ages!

My most recent position was teaching Spanish II and Spanish III at Marin Academy in San Rafael, CA for 4 years and before moving to the West Coast, I was an elementary and middle school Spanish at the Amos A. Lawrence School in Brookline, MA for 15 years.

Prior to moving to Boston, I taught in Peru, Argentina and Baltimore, MD.

When I am not teaching, I love to read, cook, go on hiking and biking adventures, and spend time with my sidekick, Panela.

Con un abrazo fuerte,

Cancellation Policy

  • Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice will incur 100% of the session fee.
  • No-Shows: If a student does not show up within 15 minutes of the start time without notice, the session will be billed as a full-fee cancellation.
  • Emergencies: In the event of an emergency or power failure (winter in California!), please try your best to communicate with me as soon as possible so we can reschedule.

Billing

Invoices will be sent at the end of each month and can be paid via Venmo or Zelle. Alternative payment methods can be used if online payment is not preferred.

Teacher & School Collaboration

If it is helpful during the school year, I am happy to collaborate with the student's teacher via email, Canvas, or a similar LMS.
